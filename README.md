# sanjose-ai-FitTrack
Difficulty tracking personal training clients’ progress, workouts, and nutrition, leading to lower client engagement and retention.

Client: Personal Trainers, Client Retention

Team: Ahmed Altameemi, Riya Murdeshwar Oct–Dec 2026

My part: I identified the client retention problem based on my experience as a personal trainer and am developing an AI-powered client management app.

The problem

Personal trainers often struggle to keep track of their clients' workouts, nutrition, body measurements, and overall progress. When this information isn't organized, it becomes harder to provide personalized support, which can lead to lower client engagement and retention.

What we built

A client management platform that allows trainers to track workouts, nutrition, weight, body measurements, and fitness goals in one place.

A dashboard where trainers can view each client's progress, training history, and areas that need improvement.

An AI assistant designed to identify clients who may be losing motivation and suggest ways to improve engagement, while the trainer makes the final decisions.

What I would do next

One challenge is making sure the AI accurately identifies clients who may be at risk of quitting. I would improve this by testing the app with real trainers and using their feedback to make the AI recommendations more accurate and useful.
